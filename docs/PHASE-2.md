# Dukkan release and Phase 2

Updated 7 October 2026. Current user scope supersedes earlier provider preferences and narrow demonstration plans.

## Core release

Make the existing founder and business workspace work end to end through hosted API inference, independently of a Codex subscription. Preserve existing ideas, conversations, documents and history.

| Core area | Acceptance required |
| --- | --- |
| Business definition | Conversation creates reviewable brief proposals; only explicit acceptance saves them. |
| Kuwait research | Market and official guidance retrieval remain separate; citations retain dates, provenance and limitations. |
| Idea critique | Evidence-readiness score, reasons, unknowns and practical options; no invented success probability. |
| Validation | Proposed tests include audience, method and advance decision rule; results and decisions preserve actual versus simulated evidence. |
| Finance | KWD scenarios, costs, transactions, budgets, CSV review and charts; estimates and simulations do not inflate recorded actuals. |
| Customers and sales | Contacts, leads, proposals and follow-ups with reviewable status and history. |
| People | Roles, candidates and onboarding records; no invented hiring outcome. |
| Operations | Suppliers, quotes, orders, tasks and decisions; no automatic purchasing. |
| Licences and documents | Source-backed preparation, supported official PDF filling, inspection, revision, exact-hash review and export; submission remains a separate action. |
| Persistence and control | Save/reload, immutable document versions, record history, archive/restore, isolation, cancellation and visible errors. |
| API independence | API-only configuration, bounded requests and prices, no local model or silent Codex fallback. |
| Public repository | Sanitised reproducible source, setup, screenshots, architecture and truthful feature status; no private assessment form or credentials. |

An incomplete core capability stays a release gap. It must not be relabelled Phase 2 to imply completion.

## Competitive advantage to demonstrate

Dukkan connects captured Kuwait evidence to a persistent business context, test results, decisions, cost assumptions, reviewable records and versioned documents. The founder can continue from the previous decision rather than recreate context in each conversation. Evidence is attributable and assumptions remain distinct from observations.

Acceptance must demonstrate that continuity across research, critique, testing, costs and preparation. Market superiority or a defensible moat is not established by a working prototype; comparison with general chatbots, templates and advisers remains a customer-research task.

## Phase 2: later scope

The user explicitly confirmed the marketplace and wider platform ideas belong here on 7 October 2026. These are planned capabilities, not live integrations.

| Later capability | Scope and prerequisite |
| --- | --- |
| Marketplace | Discover and compare verified service providers, advisers, mentors, partners and investor opportunities. Define supply, verification, matching, incentives and consent before launch. |
| Investor and partner matching | Business profiles, criteria and reviewed introductions; no assumed investor access or funding commitments. |
| Crowdfunding and funding routes | Funding-readiness preparation and referrals to authorised providers; assess each route and applicable requirements before implementing transactions. |
| Agentic marketing | Campaign drafting, approved publishing and bounded experiments; connect authorised accounts and control spend. |
| Connected business data | Sales/orders, expenses, invoices, CRM and supplier data with source, period, reconciliation and sync status. |
| POS and business-tool integrations | Provider onboarding and reviewed imports first; connectors require confirmed APIs and permissions. |
| Connected email | App-owned Gmail OAuth, exact reviewed drafts, cancellation, refresh/revoke and duplicate/ambiguous-send controls. |
| Controlled browser agents | Product-owned browser runtime, visible action log, cancellation and human gates for consequential actions. |
| Official route handoffs | Service-specific eligibility, authorised access and receipt reconciliation; preparation alone does not establish government integration. |
| Team and cloud operation | Accounts, permissions, secure multi-user persistence, backups and deployment. GitHub publication does not host the backend. |
| Live local intelligence | News and location-specific data with freshness and comparable coverage. |
| Market simulation | Explicit what-if assumptions; never represent synthetic people or scenarios as validated customer demand. |
| Incubator/accelerator support | Milestones and mentor review, subject to actual adviser or programme relationships. |
| Further languages | Expand beyond English and Arabic after demand and translation quality are established. |

Keep future previews visibly labelled, with separate implementation and acceptance status. Prioritisation within Phase 2 follows evidence from core users and confirmed partner access.
